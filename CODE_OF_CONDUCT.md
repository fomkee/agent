# Code of Conduct

We want Fomkee Agent to be a welcoming, constructive project.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and give
feedback that helps others improve the work. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Fomkee maintainers. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect
the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
